# TucuWallet World review submission draft

Status: ready for owner approval after the final Rewards UI localization and
transaction-state polish. Do not submit before that front-end change is live.

## English — submission changelog

TucuWallet expands its approved Mini App with a non-custodial World Chain
wallet and secure TUCU token rewards.

### Wallet

- World users can view their TUCU, WLD and USDC balances.
- Users can send supported tokens to another World Chain address or a resolved
  TucuWallet/World username.
- Every transfer is explicitly confirmed by the user through World Wallet.
- Transfers use `TucuTransferRouter`, a verified non-custodial contract. It
  distributes the complete approved amount to the recipient and treasury in
  the same transaction and never retains user balances.
- The router fee is 0.5% and has an immutable hard cap of 1%.

### TUCU rewards

- Eligible signed-in World users can claim one daily TUCU reward.
- Claims use short-lived EIP-712 authorizations generated by the TucuWallet
  backend and enforced by `RewardsDistributorV2` on World Chain.
- Claim identifiers cannot be reused. Mission limits, a 24-hour daily cooldown
  and a maximum of 100 TUCU per user in each 30-day window are enforced
  on-chain.
- The daily reward does not require proof of personhood. World ID remains
  available for optional or protected actions elsewhere in the Mini App.

### Security and verification

- The wallet is non-custodial. TucuWallet does not store private keys or seed
  phrases and cannot move funds without user confirmation.
- Production contracts are verified on WorldScan.
- Public source code: https://github.com/matiiiastorres/tucuwallet-contracts
- The automated Solidity suite contains 23 passing tests with 98.48% line
  coverage and 97.16% statement coverage.
- The production Rewards V2 deployment passed the repository's read-only
  on-chain audit with all eight configured missions.

### Contracts called by the Mini App

- TUCU proxy: `0x2dCFE82458e2dc32E1035c6F6535B496B04Ab3a3`
- TucuTransferRouter: `0xB1d58EF2f8bfaA0aB6F0B881aC7d8c9bcB90296f`
- RewardsDistributorV2: `0x3f350C8Bc884189194186523D79a53D93095d846`
- WLD: `0x2cfc85d8e48f8eab294be644d9e25c3030863003`
- USDC: `0x79A02482A880bCE3F13e09Da970dC34db4CD24d1`
